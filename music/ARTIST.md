# Ash Lune

**Genre:** moody alternative hip-hop, late-night R&B
**Bio:** Ash Lune makes music for 2 a.m.: rain on the window, a phone glowing in the dark,
and the people we only know through a screen. Rap verses with falsetto hooks over dreamy piano and soft 808s.
